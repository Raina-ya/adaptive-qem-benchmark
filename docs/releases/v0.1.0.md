# v0.1.0 Simulator Benchmark Release Candidate

This release candidate freezes the simulator-first adaptive-QEM study across GHZ and
fixed-seed hardware-efficient circuit families.

Included evidence:

- repeat-level GHZ and hardware-efficient volumetric results;
- raw, readout-mitigated, and linear-ZNE comparisons under fixed shot budgets;
- paired BCa uncertainty, Wilson decision-error intervals, and explicit overhead;
- cost-aware oracle labels with practical ties and inconclusive cells;
- a frozen explainable selector with in-distribution and noise-shift evaluation;
- immutable failure rows, run logs, tests, and deterministic artifact verification.

All claims remain simulator-only. Negative gains, abstentions, failures, and uncertain
winners are retained rather than filtered.

## Verified results

- 144 operating cells: 103 practical ties, 18 readout winners, 14 ZNE winners, and
  9 inconclusive cells.
- Frozen 29-cell holdout: 41.4% decision compatibility, mean RMSE regret 0.0207,
  and median regret 0.000397.
- Four unseen noise shifts: 50.0% decision compatibility, mean regret 0.00771,
  and median regret 0.
- 99 explicit hardware-efficient readout failures and 179/432 negative RMSE gains
  are preserved.

The selector is therefore a reproducible diagnostic baseline, not a validated
adaptive policy.
